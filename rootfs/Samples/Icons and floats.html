Floats

Floated right. This note floats beside the text, which wraps between the picture and the note.

The picture on the left is floated, so these lines wrap around it instead of starting below it. Text keeps flowing in the space that is left between the floats, line after line, until it passes their bottoms and returns to the full width of the column. That is how articles put photos beside paragraphs, and how older sites built their columns.

A second paragraph continues the flow. Once the floats end, lines use the whole width again, as you can see here where the text runs all the way across the card from one side to the other.
