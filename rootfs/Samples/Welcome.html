Text

Paragraphs wrap to the window width. Text can be bold, italic, underlined, monospaced, small or coloured. Accented letters work: café, naïve, Zürich, São Paulo, © 2026 – and “smart quotes” stay smart.

This paragraph has a background colour set by a CSS class.

Text is Unicode, not just Latin. Arabic is shaped and runs right to left: العربية: مرحبًا بالعالم. Hebrew too: שלום עולם. Numbers and Latin inside Arabic keep their own direction: الإصدار 7 من WRLD OS.

If you can read this, CSS display:none is not working.

A blockquote is indented on both sides. “The world is a book, and those who do not travel read only one page.”
