Personalize it your way
THE WRLD OS gives you a lot of choice in how it looks, starting before you even sign in. With the Logon Skin Maker and the Themer you can design your own logon screen, and visual styles, colors and wallpapers change everything else.
Personalization is where Windows always held back, so here you get the chance to theme your computer exactly how you like it, without any hassle.

Experience performance like never before
You don't have to put up with the slowdowns of Windows 11 here. THE WRLD OS is written from scratch, so it is light on memory and starts without compromise.
Plus, its menus are snappy: they open the moment you click.

A consistent set of menus
Your WRLD OS settings live in one place: the Control Panel. You don't have to switch between two different settings apps or go on a scavenger hunt.
Every option has one home, grouped by what it is for.

Getting back the old days
Remember when computers used to be fun? THE WRLD OS comes with a set of games: Orbit Pinball, WRLD Sentinel (a first-person shooter), Solitaire, Snake, Pong and Minesweeper.
THE WRLD Media Player plays almost anything you give it, from MP3, Ogg Vorbis, FLAC and WAV music to MPEG video, with visualizations and skins.

A compatibility beast
Want your Mac, Linux and Windows programs at the same time? Look no further. THE WRLD OS runs Windows .exe programs, opens Mac .dmg disk images and .app bundles, and installs Linux .deb and .rpm packages.
So you don't really need to switch computers.

Online from the first start
The network adapters of real PCs and virtual machines work as soon as THE WRLD OS starts, with no drivers to download.
THE WRLD Browser brings secure HTTPS sites and a built-in ad blocker, and Network Connections shows your connection's status at a glance.

All your discs and drives
Explorer opens hard disks, USB flash drives, CDs, DVDs and Blu-ray discs. Insert a disc or plug in a drive and AutoPlay asks what to do with it.
Everything you change is saved to your hard disk.

No mouse? No problem
Every window, menu and dialog works from the keyboard: Tab moves between controls, Alt opens the menu bar and the Windows key opens the Start menu.
If no mouse is connected, Mouse Keys turns on by itself so the number pad moves the pointer.
